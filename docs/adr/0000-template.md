# NNNN. Short title in the imperative or as a statement

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who agreed to this
- **Related:** PRD requirement IDs, issues, other ADRs

## Context

What is the problem, and what forces shape it? State the constraints that make
this a real decision rather than an obvious one: performance targets, ecosystem
compatibility, the skills of the people who will maintain it, deadlines, upstream
limitations.

Write this section so that someone who disagrees can see exactly which assumption
they would have to attack.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two sentences, in the active voice. Then the detail:
the shape of the API, the package, the configuration, the file layout — whatever
someone implementing this needs.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, and what becomes harder. Be explicit about the costs; a
decision record that lists only benefits is a sales sheet, not a record.

Include the follow-up work the decision creates, and what would make the team
revisit it.

## Alternatives considered

For each alternative: what it was, why it was rejected, and whether it stays
viable as a future option (a decision that is reversible is often worth taking).

The point of this section is that the *next* person does not re-open a settled
question without knowing what was already weighed.
